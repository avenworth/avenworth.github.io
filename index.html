FAQ

Who do you work with?

Sole traders and small owner-operated businesses — trades, services and hospitality — using Xero or QuickBooks Online. We work fully remotely, Australia-wide, from Thornlands QLD.

Can you help with BAS or tax?

We do bookkeeping, not tax. GST, BAS and tax decisions and lodgements stay with your registered tax or BAS agent, and we work alongside them. We are not a registered tax or BAS agent.

How are fees agreed?

After a quick look at the volume and condition of your records. You get a fixed price and a written scope before any work starts, and nothing extra happens without your approval.
